## Question

What question does this report investigate?

## Findings

Separate documented facts, attributed claims, and your interpretation. Link each
important claim to its source.

## Limitations

Record the evidence cutoff, gaps in the research, and claims that need rechecking.

## Sources

Use standard Markdown links to primary sources, with access dates where useful.
